              The OCD document workbench

              The native surface of the OCD — the Open Canonical Document, one normalized model a document is lifted into once and projected from many times. Open an OCD-EPUB and its pages are served as they are; drop a PDF and it is lifted into the same model first.

              Nothing is uploaded
              The book is unzipped in your browser and served to the pages by a Service Worker.
              One model, many projections
              PDF, EPUB, HTML, SVG, Markdown, DocTags — every one of them is written from the same OCD, so what you read here is what any of them will carry.
              Built on the qry stack
              Zero-dependency DOM core, Shoelace components, Lucide icons.
